# Three-minute demo

## Before going on stage

Do these in the hour before. Rehearse the whole path once on this exact build.

- [ ] Phone charged and unplugged from the Mac.
- [ ] Volume up. The ringer switch doesn't matter.
- [ ] Do Not Disturb on, so no notification talks over Juno.
- [ ] On cellular or your own hotspot, not the venue Wi-Fi.
- [ ] Vapi has credit (the voice agent stops without it).
- [ ] Signed in to Juno with your own account.
- [ ] VoiceOver off, unless you have rehearsed this exact build with it on.
- [ ] Camera permission granted: open the camera once (swipe down on the feed) and choose Allow when iOS asks.
- [ ] Picture descriptions allowed. The one-time question can only be answered by the VoiceOver action Allow descriptions, or by drawing a clockwise circle inside the camera to wake the agent and saying "yes". Do it in the same rehearsal.
- [ ] Ten to fifteen minutes before going on: post one good photo from your own account, then open Juno and listen to the top few posts once. That fetches their picture descriptions ahead of time, so the first post isn't six seconds late on stage.
- [ ] Pick the first post you will like and make sure you haven't liked it already: a like on a liked post removes it.
- [ ] Put one simple, well-lit object on the table (a mug works), for the camera.

### How to do the gestures

- **Next and previous:** short, crisp taps on the bottom half (next) or top half (previous), or swipes right and left. Never flick up or down to scroll: a flick up opens the replies and a flick down opens the camera.
- **Like:** put two fingers down together, keep them still, and keep holding through the light buzz until the like sound plays, about a second and a half.
- **Circle:** one finger, one large, complete loop at a steady speed in the middle of the screen. Clockwise wakes the agent; counter-clockwise puts it to sleep.
- **While the agent is awake, don't tap the screen.** A bottom-half tap ends the agent and opens the account screen, and a top-half tap reads the help. Talk, then draw the counter-clockwise circle to go back to scrolling.

## The script

About three minutes. Each step is what you do, what the room hears, and one line you can say while it happens.

0. **Optional opening, 20 seconds, only if you have rehearsed it.** Practice the gestures is a button on the account screen (wake the agent with a clockwise circle, then tap the bottom half). Choose it and do a step or two with your eyes closed, then finish or skip practice and choose Back to the feed.
   - Heard: Juno asks for each gesture and says whether it worked.
   - Say: "A blind user learns Juno the way I'm doing it now: by ear."

1. **Open Juno** and let the first post play to the end.
   - Heard: who posted and when, the post's words, then its picture described.
   - Say: "Juno reads Bluesky one post at a time, and describes every picture, because most posts don't have alt text."

2. **Tap the bottom half** for the next post, then once more. Tap the top half to go back one.
   - Heard: a light tap and the next post, read from the start.
   - Say: "No buttons to find: the bottom half goes forward and the top half goes back."

3. **Hold two fingers** on a post you haven't liked until the like sound plays.
   - Heard: a light buzz, then three rising notes and "Liked."
   - Say: "Liking is a deliberate two-finger hold, so it never happens by accident."

4. **Draw a clockwise circle.**
   - Heard: "Waking the agent", then "I'm listening."
   - Say: "Now I can just talk to it."

5. **Say "Post: hello from the hackathon, I'm posting this with my voice."** Wait for the read-back, then say **"post it"**.
   - Heard: "Posting to your followers: hello from the hackathon… Shall I post it?", then "Posting in five seconds. Say undo to stop it."
   - Say: "Nothing goes out until Juno reads it back and I say yes, and I still have five seconds to undo."

6. **Draw a counter-clockwise circle**, let the post start reading, then **swipe down**.
   - Heard: "Agent is asleep. Back to scrolling.", the post, then "Camera…" and how to use it.
   - Say: "The camera helps me take a picture I can't see."

7. **Point the back camera at the object on the table**, not at the audience, and hold still until Juno says to take the picture. **Tap the bottom half.**
   - Heard: framing cues such as "Move left" or "Hold steady", ticks that speed up near the center, the shutter, then what is in the picture and "Keep it or take another?"
   - Say: "It tells me how to aim, then what I actually got."

8. **Tap the bottom half to keep it.** Juno goes back to the feed by itself and wakes the agent. Wait for the question.
   - Heard: "Keeping it.", then "Your photo is ready to post. What should the post say?"
   - Say: "Keeping the photo hands it straight to the agent."

9. **Say what the post should say**, listen to the read-back, then say **"post it"**.
   - Heard: "Posting to your followers: … With the image description: … Shall I post it?", then the five-second notice.
   - Say: "And the photo goes out with a description, so the next blind person to see it hears it too."

10. **Draw a counter-clockwise circle** to finish on the feed.
    - Say: "That's Juno."

If you open the camera and decide not to keep a photo, swipe up (or draw a counter-clockwise circle) to go back to the feed.

## If something goes wrong

- **The agent won't connect** ("I couldn't connect"): close Juno completely and reopen it, then draw the circle again.
- **A picture description is slow** ("being described" or nothing yet): tap the bottom half and move on to the next post.
- **The camera asks about sending pictures to OpenAI:** wake the agent with a clockwise circle and say "yes".
- **No network** ("Can't reach Bluesky" at launch): choose Retry once; if it still fails, choose Listen without signing in to show the public feed, and skip the posting steps.
- **A like removes a like** ("Like removed."): that post was already liked; hold two fingers again to like it.
- **A gesture isn't recognized** (two quick ticks and "Gesture not recognized"): do it again, larger and steadier.
- **The account screen opened** (a tap while the agent was awake): choose Back to the feed and draw the clockwise circle again.
